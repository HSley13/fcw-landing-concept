Good to know

FAQ

Still have a question? Stop by during shop hours or send us a message.

How do I donate a bike or parts?

Drop it off during shop hours, no appointment needed. If you can’t get it to us, reach out and we’ll set up a pickup.

Are donations tax-deductible?

Yes. FCW is a registered nonprofit. Ask for a receipt when you drop items off.

Does FCW give away free bikes?

Through specific programs, yes: bikes for free or close to free for community members in need. We also sell low-cost “as-is” bikes and refurbished bikes to keep the shop going.

Do you buy or trade bikes?

No. We run on donated bikes, parts and accessories.

Can I volunteer?

Absolutely. Skilled mechanic or total beginner, there’s a way to help. Sign up through our volunteer portal.

Where does the money go?

All proceeds from bike and parts sales go back into our community programs and keeping the shop running.
